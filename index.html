Why Better Human

Healthcare is reactive. Prevention should be continuous.

Most people and organizations are stuck with fragmented data, late interventions, and no unified system to act early.

What’s broken

Data is scattered across labs, wearables, and visits. Insights arrive too late, and action plans are inconsistent.

Our approach

A “Health OS” that turns biomarkers into risk visibility + clear next steps—supported by clinical oversight and modern workflows.

FAQ

Quick answers.

Keep this tight. The goal is to reduce objections, not explain every detail.

Is this medical care? ▾

Better Human provides biomarker-based insights and action planning. Clinical involvement depends on your program design and regulatory posture. (As you finalize scope, refine this language with counsel.)

How do you handle privacy? ▾

Privacy-first by design: minimal collection, encrypted storage/transit, and role-based access. For employer programs, reporting should be aggregated and designed to avoid exposing individual identities.

What’s required to pilot for employers? ▾

A short discovery call, pilot goals, cohort size, and reporting needs. We’ll propose a lightweight implementation plan and success metrics.
